How it’s made

Engine

reel/showreel.js draws every frame as a pure function of time on a single Canvas 2D context — no libraries, no assets beyond two open-source fonts.

Render

tools/render.js drives headless Chromium and averages 8 sub-frames per output frame across a 180° shutter, so the motion blur comes from real movement. ffmpeg adds film grain and encodes the result.

Score

tools/soundtrack.py synthesizes kick, bass, supersaw pads, FM plucks, risers and every UI tick with numpy, placed on the same beat timeline as the picture.
